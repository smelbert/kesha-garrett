About

Strategy Meets Execution

For nearly two decades, I have worked at the intersection of strategy and execution across civic institutions, community-based organizations, and the private sector.

As Chief Operating Officer of the Columbus Urban League, I serve as senior executive partner to the President & CEO. Brought on to lead human resources, technology, facilities, and cross-functional operations, my role expanded to full financial oversight, including the budget, annual audit, Form 990, cash flow, and compliance, across annual operating budgets of $11M–$18M.

Before rejoining the Urban League, I served as COO of Besa Community. Earlier, I rose from Executive Assistant to Chief of Staff to the President & CEO of the Columbus Urban League, and built operating systems in the civic and private sectors with the Columbus Partnership and IKE Smart City.

“People are the most important part of any organization. Strong systems are what make mission and performance possible.”Kesha S. Garrett

Organizational Stabilization

Stabilizing every department

Led a strategic plan to stabilize every department after a surge of largely pass-through funding outpaced the organization’s infrastructure, sustaining operations as the organization right-sized, with clean audits every year.

People & Culture

A place people want to stay

Led a culture shift that reduced turnover, overhauled HR policies and benefits with cost savings, elevated HR into a VP-led People & Organizational Effectiveness function, and promoted talent from within.

Capital Investment

Transforming the building

Funded a full renovation of the first and second floors, including energy-efficient lighting and wiring, with funding from the City of Columbus and the State of Ohio, plus previously awarded federal funds that prior leadership had been unable to access.
